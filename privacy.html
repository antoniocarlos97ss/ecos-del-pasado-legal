Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This policy describes how ecosupload ("the app"), a private tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel Ecos del Pasado, handles data.

Who uses the app

The app is used only by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and does not collect data from other people.

Google user data accessed

With the owner's authorization, the app uses the YouTube Data API scopes youtube.upload and youtube to upload videos, set thumbnails, titles, descriptions, tags and publication schedules, and to read the channel's basic information to confirm the correct channel.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to perform these uploads on the owner's channel. The OAuth token is stored privately on the owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API use is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
